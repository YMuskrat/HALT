# Attribution and review policy

Original HALT implementation: HALT contributors.

Research attribution is separate:

| Method | Research authors |
|---|---|
| HALT-CoT | Yassir Laaouach |
| ThinkBrake v1 | Minjae Oh, Sangjun Song, Seungkyu Lee, Sungmin Jo, Yohan Jo |
| Answer Convergence | Xin Liu, Lu Wang |
| REFRAIN | Renliang Sun, Wei Cheng, Dawei Li, Haifeng Chen, Wei Wang |
| DEER | Chenxu Yang, Qingyi Si, Yongjie Duan, Zheliang Zhu, Chenyu Zhu, Zheng Lin, Li Cao, Weiping Wang |

Canonical references, paper versions, repository commits and source relationships
are in [SOURCE_LEDGER.md](SOURCE_LEDGER.md) and `method_cards/*.json`.
HALT-CoT remains a separately credited method within the broader HALT library.
HALT does not claim to have invented early stopping or to be its first framework.

All method cards currently say **author review: unreviewed**. No authors were
contacted and no endorsement is implied. A review should identify the reviewer,
date, exact recipe, model/backend, reviewed artifact and scope. One configuration's
review cannot validate every backend or reproduce a paper's aggregate results.

Cite the HALT software when using its runtime and independently cite every research
method used. Citation of HALT alone does not credit the original stopping methods.
No upstream algorithm files or downloaded model weights are redistributed in this
package. Independent implementations preserve source references; any future direct
code reuse must retain its original license notices.
